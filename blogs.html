Frequently Asked Questions About Signage

How much does an LED sign board cost in Hyderabad?

LED sign board prices in Hyderabad typically range from ₹850 to ₹4,000 per square foot. The exact cost depends on material quality, LED type (standard/premium/RGB), installation complexity, and warranty duration. For example, a basic ACP LED board starts at ₹850/sqft, while premium stainless steel 3D letters with RGB lighting can go up to ₹4,000/sqft.

What is the installation timeline for signage?

Our standard installation process takes 7-9 working days after design approval. This includes in-house manufacturing, quality checks, and professional installation. For large terrace signage projects on high-rise buildings, the timeline may extend to 30-90 working days depending on structural requirements and municipal permissions.

Do you provide site visits in Andhra Pradesh?

Yes, we offer free site visits across all major cities in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Our team visits your location to take precise measurements, assess visibility requirements, understand structural considerations, and provide expert recommendations before providing a quotation.

What warranty do you provide on LED signage?

We offer flexible warranty options: 3 years (standard), 5 years (recommended), and 10 years (premium). All warranties include LED replacement, SMPS coverage, and free service support. We also provide Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) after warranty expiration for continued support.
