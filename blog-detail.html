How to Choose the Right LED Signage for Your Business in Hyderabad

Rakesh Adepu
Founder & CEO, V SIGN
March 15, 2024
Share:
Choosing the right signage can significantly impact your business visibility

Introduction

Selecting the right LED signage for your business in Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Vizag, or anywhere in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana is a critical decision that impacts your brand visibility, customer attraction, and overall business success. With over a decade of experience in signage manufacturing and installation, I've seen businesses make costly mistakes by choosing the wrong signage type or material.

This comprehensive guide will walk you through the key considerations for choosing the perfect signage solution for your business needs, budget, and location.

1. Understand Your Location Requirements

The first and most important consideration is your signage's location. Different locations have different requirements:

Terrace/Rooftop Signage

Best for: High-rise buildings, hotels, corporate offices

  • Requires wind load calculations (150+ kmph)
  • Needs high-brightness LEDs for long-distance visibility
  • Structural engineering approval often required
  • Longer installation timeline (30-90 days)

Shop Front/Facade Signage

Best for: Retail stores, restaurants, showrooms

  • Focus on aesthetic appeal and branding
  • Consider pedestrian and vehicular visibility
  • Quick installation (7-9 days typical)
  • Regular cleaning and maintenance needed

Wayfinding/Directional Signage

Best for: Hospitals, campuses, malls, complexes

  • Prioritize clarity and readability
  • ADA compliance for accessibility
  • Durable materials for high-traffic areas
  • Consistent design system across locations

3. LED Type and Brightness Considerations

The type of LED you choose affects visibility, energy consumption, and maintenance:

Standard White LEDs

Brightness: 5,000-7,000 nits

Power: 15-20W per sqft

Best for: Indoor signage, shop fronts

Cost: ₹200-300 per sqft

High-Brightness LEDs

Brightness: 8,000-10,000 nits

Power: 25-35W per sqft

Best for: Terrace signage, highways

Cost: ₹400-600 per sqft

RGB Color LEDs

Brightness: 6,000-8,000 nits

Power: 30-40W per sqft

Best for: Hotels, entertainment

Cost: ₹500-800 per sqft

Brightness Guideline: For Hyderabad's sunny conditions, we recommend minimum 8,000 nits for outdoor signage. In areas with less direct sunlight, 5,000-6,000 nits may suffice.

4. Size and Placement Strategy

Getting the size right is crucial for visibility and impact:

Viewing Distance Formula

Letter height (in inches) = Viewing distance (in feet) / 10

Example: For 100 feet viewing distance, minimum letter height should be 10 inches (100/10)

Common Size Recommendations

  • Shop boards: 4x2 ft to 8x4 ft
  • Terrace signage: 10x3 ft to 20x6 ft
  • Building name: 12x4 ft to 30x8 ft
  • Directional signs: 2x1 ft to 4x2 ft

Case Study: Restaurant Signage in Vijayawada

A popular restaurant on MG Road, Vijayawada, increased foot traffic by 40% after optimizing their signage:

6. Maintenance and Warranty Considerations

Proper maintenance extends signage life and maintains appearance:

Regular Cleaning

Dust and pollution in cities like Hyderabad and Vizag can reduce LED brightness by 20-30% in 6 months. Quarterly cleaning maintains optimal visibility.

Annual Maintenance Contracts

AMC costs 15-20% of initial investment annually but includes regular checks, cleaning, and priority service. Recommended for terrace and high-rise signage.

Warranty Selection

We recommend 5-year warranty for most businesses. For high-value investments or hard-to-access locations, consider 10-year warranty for peace of mind.

Conclusion

Choosing the right LED signage involves careful consideration of location, material, size, LED type, budget, and maintenance requirements. By following this guide and working with experienced signage professionals like V SIGN, you can make an informed decision that delivers maximum visibility and return on investment for your business.

Key Takeaways:

  1. Assess your location and viewing requirements first
  2. Choose materials based on durability needs and budget
  3. Select LED brightness appropriate for your environment
  4. Size your signage based on viewing distance
  5. Plan for maintenance from the beginning
  6. Consider long-term ROI, not just initial cost
