How much does signage cost per square foot? +
Prices typically range from ₹800 to ₹4,000 per sq ft. Factors include material, lighting, height, city and installation complexity.

Is the budget planner price final? +
No. The planner gives a realistic price range. Final pricing is confirmed after site inspection and measurements.
Do you provide installation services? +
Yes. We provide end-to-end service including fabrication, transport and installation.
Do prices vary by city or location? +
Yes. Factors like access height, permissions, logistics and manpower affect pricing.
What warranty do you provide? +
We offer 3-year, 5-year and 10-year warranties depending on product type.

We guide you through required permissions. Final approvals depend on local authorities.
